Quran memorization

Wirdee AI

A calm, local-first Quran memorization app. Your progress stays on your device, with optional backup to iCloud, Google Drive, or both.

What the app does

Wirdee AI helps you memorize and review Quran pages with listening, recitation, progress tracking, and daily goals. It is designed to be useful offline and without an account.

Cloud backup is optional

On iPhone, you can enable iCloud backup, connect Google Drive, or use both. Google Drive access is requested only when you choose to connect it, and it is used only for your own backup and recording files.
