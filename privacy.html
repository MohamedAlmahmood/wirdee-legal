Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Wirdee AI is a Quran memorization app operated by Mohamed Almahmood. This policy explains how the app handles your information. Questions and data requests can be sent to almahmood.mo@gmail.com.

Information the app uses

The app stores memorization progress, reading position, practice history, settings, and recordings on your device. This information can reveal information about your religious practice. Wirdee AI does not require an account and does not operate a server that receives your learning records.

iCloud

On iPhone, you can enable iCloud backup. The app writes backup data to its iCloud container using the Apple account already configured on your device. Apple processes iCloud data under Apple's terms and privacy policy. You can turn iCloud backup off in Wirdee AI Settings.

Google Drive is optional

You can connect Google Drive to keep a second copy of your progress or to restore it on another device. You can use iCloud, Google Drive, or both. Google Drive is never required to use the app.

If you choose Connect Google Drive, the app opens Google's authorization page in the system browser and requests only:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata, which lets the app create and manage its own hidden backup files in your Google Drive.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which lets the app create and manage recording files and folders that you choose to keep in Google Drive.

The app also receives your Google account identifier and email address so it can show which account is connected and prevent one account's backup from being written into another account's Drive.

How Google user data is used

Wirdee AI uses Google Drive only to:

  • Store your memorization progress, settings, and related backup metadata in the app's hidden Google Drive app-data folder.
  • Read, update, or remove those backup files when you sync.
  • Create and manage recording files and folders that you explicitly choose to store in Google Drive.
  • Detect an expired or revoked authorization so the app can ask you to reconnect.

Google user data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. It is not shared with data brokers and is not sent to a Wirdee AI backend. Wirdee AI does not request access to Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, Google Photos, or unrelated Google Drive files.

Wirdee AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Credentials

Google OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on your device in the iOS Keychain through the app's secure storage API. Tokens are not included in progress exports, cloud backup files, logs, or analytics.

Disconnecting and deleting data

You can disconnect Google Drive from Wirdee AI Settings at any time. Disconnecting removes the Google tokens from the device. It does not automatically delete backup files already stored in Google Drive.

You can remove Wirdee AI's access from your Google Account permissions page. Removing access stops future authorization but does not itself delete files already in Drive.

The app's Delete my data action removes local progress and requests deletion of the app's backup files from every connected cloud destination. Recording files in visible cloud folders remain until you delete them from the app or the cloud provider.

Retention and third parties

Local data remains until you reset it, delete it, or uninstall the app. iCloud data is controlled by your Apple account. Google Drive data is controlled by your Google account. Apple and Google process data under their own privacy policies when you use their services.

Wirdee AI does not sell or rent personal information and does not use learning records for targeted advertising.

Children

Wirdee AI is not directed to children under 13. Do not use the app to submit personal information if you are under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new date. Continued use of the app after an update means you should review the revised policy.
